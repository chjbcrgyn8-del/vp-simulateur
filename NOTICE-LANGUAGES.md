# Simulator language editions

The French pages are the reference edition. The English interface uses a local dictionary: no page content, form inputs or case data is submitted to a translation service. The English video editions have their own English visuals, voiceover, posters and caption tracks. The English edition includes localised Vella Partners field sheets, import templates and human-readable reports. User-entered information, machine-readable backup identifiers and third-party evidence remain in their original form. The original French media and downloads are retained.

Initial English wording was generated offline using the Argos French-to-English package (1.9), based on OPUS-MT by Jörg Tiedemann and Santhosh Thottingal, with editorial changes to launcher and industry terminology.
